**Version:** v1.0

**Status:** RESET PACKAGE COMPLETE — approval required before UX redesign

## Decision

Phase 5 v0.1–v0.5 is no longer the controlling design direction.

Those prototypes are retained as archived design experiments.

The project is reset around the original objective and the author's clarified professional purpose:

> present the groundwater recharge study as evidence of a long-running professional body of work in soil and water conservation engineering, GIS, groundwater recharge planning, field implementation and community-informed decision-making.

## New controlling Phase 5 documents

1. `Professional_Legacy_Story_Architecture_v1.0.md`
2. `Visual_Storyboard_Professional_Legacy_v1.0.md`
3. `Professional_Audience_Brand_Brief_v1.0.md`

## Design rule

No new UX prototype should be produced until the three documents above are reviewed and approved.

## Major corrections

- story first, interface second;
- professional body of work replaces guide-first framing;
- Sabarkantha is the applied landscape, not just a dataset;
- 58 constructed structures become evidence of practice;
- real maps/photos/sections become the dominant visual language;
- card-heavy dashboard styling is retired;
- methodological qualifications move to proportionate notes;
- lineaments are interpreted hydrogeologically;
- personal branding is restrained but clearly connected to the body of work;
- outcomes will be shown when evidence is available and classified by evidence type.
