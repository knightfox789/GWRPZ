# Research Reference Register — GWRPZ Web Article

**Version:** v0.1

**Purpose:** Curated research base for the practitioner evidence story.

## R01 — GIS-MCDA foundation

**Malczewski, J. (2006).** GIS-based multicriteria decision analysis: a survey of the literature. *International Journal of Geographical Information Science*, 20(7), 703–726.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/13658810600661508

**Relevance:** establishes GIS-MCDA as a mature spatial decision-support field and shows that weighting / decision rules explicitly combine spatial information with decision preferences.

**Article use:** Chapter 5 / Research & Method.

---

## R02 — MAR suitability mapping review

**Sallwey, J., Bonilla Valverde, J. P., Vásquez López, F., Junghanns, R., & Stefan, C. (2019).** Suitability maps for managed aquifer recharge: a review of multi-criteria decision analysis studies. *Environmental Reviews*, 27(2), 138–150.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1139/er-2018-0069

**Relevance:** review of GIS-MCDA in MAR site selection; important because criteria and weighting vary across studies.

**Article use:** supports the message that a suitability framework must be context-specific rather than copied as a universal recipe.

---

## R03 — Suitability mapping plus hydrogeological modelling

**Rahman, M. A., Rusteberg, B., Gogu, R. C., Ferreira, J. P. L., & Sauter, M. (2013).** An integrated study of spatial multicriteria analysis and mathematical modelling for managed aquifer recharge site suitability mapping and site ranking at Northern Gaza coastal aquifer. *Journal of Environmental Management*, 124, 25–39.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jenvman.2013.03.023

**Relevance:** combines spatial multicriteria suitability with groundwater modelling and site ranking.

**Article use:** strong reference for “map first, deeper hydrogeological verification next”.

---

## R04 — South India recharge structures

**Aju, C. D., Achu, A. L., Raicy, M. C., & Reghunath, R. (2021).** Identification of suitable sites and structures for artificial groundwater recharge for sustainable water resources management in Vamanapuram River Basin, South India. *HydroResearch*, 4, 24–37.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.hydres.2021.04.001

**Relevance:** links recharge-potential zonation with selection of possible recharge mechanisms / structures in an Indian basin.

**Article use:** India-specific comparison for moving from potential zones toward structure planning.

---

## R05 — Western India recharge potential

**Kadam, A. K., Umrikar, B. N., & Sankhua, R. N. (2020).** Assessment of recharge potential zones for groundwater development and management using geospatial and MCDA technologies in semiarid region of Western India. *SN Applied Sciences*, 2, 312.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s42452-020-2079-7

**Relevance:** semiarid Western India application of geospatial and MCDA recharge-potential mapping.

**Article use:** regional research context.

---

## R06 — Indian groundwater-potential GIS / AHP

**Arulbalaji, P., Padmalal, D., & Sreelash, K. (2019).** GIS and AHP techniques based delineation of groundwater potential zones: a case study from Southern Western Ghats, India. *Scientific Reports*, 9, 2082.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-38567-x

**Relevance:** multi-layer groundwater-potential mapping and validation using groundwater information.

**Article use:** demonstrates the value of combining thematic layers and validating the resulting spatial model.

---

## R07 — Global managed aquifer recharge context

**Dillon, P., Stuyfzand, P., Grischek, T., et al. (2019).** Sixty years of global progress in managed aquifer recharge. *Hydrogeology Journal*, 27, 1–30.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s10040-018-1841-z

**Relevance:** global history and progress of managed aquifer recharge; emphasises engineering, hydrogeology, water quality, governance, reliability and implementation.

**Article use:** wider context for why siting is only one component of MAR planning and operation.

---

## R08 — Recent GIS-MCDA recharge siting

**Talozi, S. A., Alharahsheh, T. A., & Hamdan, I. A. (2023).** Selecting suitable sites for groundwater recharge in Jordan using the spreading techniques via the integration of multi-criteria decision analysis and geographic information system tools. *Groundwater for Sustainable Development*, 22, 100948.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gsd.2023.100948

**Relevance:** incorporates multiple technical, environmental and economic criteria for recharge site suitability.

**Article use:** reinforces the point that criteria selection should respond to intervention method and local context.

# Recommended citation behaviour in the article

Do not insert academic citations into every paragraph.

Use three levels:

### Main story
Small superscript / “Research” marker only when a technical claim needs external context.

### Research drawer
1–3 directly relevant papers.

### Full references
Complete selected bibliography.

The Sabarkantha result numbers must cite the project’s own validated evidence, not these external papers.
